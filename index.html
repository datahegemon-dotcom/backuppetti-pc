🍓 Raspberry Pi / Linux — easiest way (also starts on boot):
Open a Terminal and paste this one line:
curl -fsSL https://datahegemon-dotcom.github.io/backuppetti-pc/install-pi.sh | sudo bash
It installs the Box and sets it to launch automatically every time the machine powers on — then open http://localhost:8088 and follow the three steps below.

Or run it by hand (any of the above): open a Terminal in your Downloads folder and run chmod +x ./backuppetti-agent-* then ./backuppetti-agent-*, then open http://localhost:8088.

On a Mac, if it says the app can't be opened, run once: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ./backuppetti-agent-*
On Linux / Pi, your USB drive is usually at a path like /media/you/USB — if it isn't in the drive list, just type that path in Step 1.

How to set it up

  1. Download the file using the green button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file (look in your Downloads folder).
  3. Windows may show a blue box saying “Windows protected your PC.” This is normal for new apps — your file is safe.
    🛡️ Windows protected your PC
    Click More info, then click Run anyway
  4. A setup page opens in your browser. Now do these three things:

On the setup page — 3 quick steps

  1. Choose your drive. Under “Step 1”, pick the drive where files should be saved — usually your USB drive or external disk (e.g. D:). Click “Use this drive”.
  2. Open or create a folder — one for each person or account (like “Amma” or “Shop”). Type a name and click “Create folder & open it” (it opens by itself), or click an existing folder to open it.
  3. Inside that folder, click the big “Generate QR code for this folder” button. The QR appears right there.
  4. On your phone, open BackupPetti → Your Box → “Scan your box's QR” and point the camera at that QR. Connected! ✅
  5. Want to add another person? Just make another folder and its own QR — each folder keeps that person's files separate.
🔒 Your files go straight from your phone to this PC over your own WiFi — they never leave your house or touch anyone's cloud.
📶 Your phone and PC must be on the same WiFi (home or shop). That's what keeps everything off the internet.
💻 Keep this app running (and your PC switched on) when you back up.
